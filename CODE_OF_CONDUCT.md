# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Cadence is a systems and measurement project. Everyone participating in its
issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces is expected to be
technical, specific, constructive, and respectful.

Expected behaviour:

- Critique code, experiments, and claims rather than people.
- Ask for configuration, seed, workload, and raw evidence when results differ.
- State uncertainty and limitations directly.
- Keep private prompts, outputs, credentials, and production traces out of
  public project spaces.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory conduct, deliberate disruption,
or publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in project spaces and when representing the project
publicly. Report security or privacy concerns through GitHub's private
vulnerability reporting. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when conduct does not meet
this standard.

This document follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1, condensed for a single-maintainer research repository.
